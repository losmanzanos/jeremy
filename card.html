What to hand the printer

Files
apex-business-card.pdf — two pages, front then back. 300dpi PNGs are included if they ask for images instead.
Trim
3.5 × 2 in, standard US card
Bleed
0.125 in on all four edges. Document is 3.75 × 2.25 in.
Safety
All type sits 0.125 in inside the trim. The dashed line above is the cut; nothing important touches it.
Color
Built in RGB. Ink #1A1A18, paper #F4F3EE. Most online printers convert for you; a trade printer may want CMYK, and these convert cleanly because the palette is near-neutral.
Fonts
Bodoni Moda and Jost, both embedded in the PDF. Nothing to install.
Stock
Uncoated or soft-touch matte. The dark face will look flat and expensive uncoated and slightly cheap on gloss.

The contour lines are real Colorado elevation data, the same survey the website is drawn from, so the card and the site are the same map at different crops.
