Terms

The short version of the rules.

What this site is, what it is not, and the practical terms that apply before and after you become a client.

Last updated September 2026

What this site is

An introduction to a private counseling practice, so you can decide whether to make contact. That is the whole purpose.

It is not clinical advice

Nothing on these pages is a diagnosis, a treatment plan, or clinical advice for your situation. The specialty pages describe how this practice tends to approach a problem, in general terms. They cannot account for you, and they are not a substitute for care.

Reading this does not make you a client

A professional relationship begins only after a consultation, a completed intake, and a signed informed consent agreement. Sending the contact form does not create one, and neither does a reply to it. Until that agreement is signed, nothing you send is protected by the confidentiality that applies to an established client.

Emergencies

This practice does not provide crisis or emergency services. Messages and voicemail are not monitored around the clock and there is no on-call coverage.

If you are in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, or call 911. In Colorado you can also reach the Crisis Line at 1-844-493-8255 or text TALK to 38255.

Colorado only

Dr. Reichert is licensed as a Professional Counselor in Colorado. Sessions are provided by telehealth to people who are physically located in Colorado at the time of the session. This is a condition of licensure, not a preference. If you move, or if you will be out of state on a session day, say so in advance and we will work out what is possible.

Telehealth

Telehealth suits a great many people and does not suit everyone. It depends on a private place to sit and a workable connection, and it is a poor container for someone who needs same-day in-person care. If telehealth is not appropriate for your situation, you will be told so and helped to find a referral.

Fees, scheduling, and cancellation

The current fee is $185 for a sixty-minute session. A sliding scale exists and is real. Several insurance plans are accepted; coverage is always worth verifying with your insurer directly, because networks change without notice.

Appointments cancelled with less than twenty-four hours' notice may be charged in full. Life happens and that is handled case by case, but the expectation is stated here so nobody is surprised by it.

Email and text

Ordinary email and SMS are not secure. They are fine for scheduling and logistics. They are not the place for clinical content, and they are not a way to reach anyone urgently.

Links out

Where this site points to an outside organization, it does so because the resource is likely to be useful. Those sites are run by other people under their own terms and privacy practices, and this practice is not responsible for them.

Content

The writing, photography, and design on this site belong to Apex Mental Health Counseling and its licensors. Quote a passage with attribution if it is useful to you. Please do not republish the site wholesale.

Changes

These terms may be updated. The date at the top shows when that last happened, and continuing to use the site means the current version applies.

Governing law

Colorado law governs these terms and the practice of counseling described here.
