The Work

A chapter closed before you were ready.

Counseling for grief, shifting faith, and life that changed shape. No stages, no closure deadline. Telehealth across Colorado.

Grief gets a script, and the script is short. People are generous for about three weeks and then the expectation quietly resets, and you are left holding something that has barely started to change shape.

Grief that is not a death

A good deal of what people bring in here has no funeral attached to it, which means it never got acknowledged as a loss in the first place. A marriage. A career that ended before you were done with it. A body that does not do what it used to. A child who is grown and gone. A friendship that simply stopped without an argument.

Unacknowledged losses do not grieve themselves. They tend to go underground and come back up as irritability, or numbness, or a level of exhaustion nobody can account for.

Faith that has shifted

This is one of the more isolating things a person can go through, because both available audiences are wrong. The people inside your tradition hear doubt as a problem to be solved, and the people outside it hear it as an overdue correction. Neither is listening.

I trained at Denver Seminary and I can sit in that conversation without steering it. If your faith is intact and central, it belongs in the work. If it has fractured, that belongs here too, and I am not going to treat either direction as the outcome we are aiming for.

Life that changed shape

Divorce, a move, retirement, an empty house, a diagnosis, a career ending on someone else's schedule. The common thread is that the story you were living inside stopped being available, and you are now expected to carry on as though a replacement were obvious.

What I will not do

  • Give you stages, or tell you where you should be in them by now
  • Move you toward closure, which is a word that mostly serves the people watching
  • Treat ongoing grief as a disorder because it has outlasted someone's expectations
  • Decide for you what any of it meant

What we do instead is more modest. We make room for it, we look at what it is asking of you, and we work on the parts of your life that have narrowed around it.
